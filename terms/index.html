SMS Terms (Mother Bumper Driving Feedback Alerts)

  • What the program is: Mother Bumper sends text messages to the parent who turns on a Mother Bumper sticker. Messages pass along anonymous feedback (praise or a concern) that other people send about that car by scanning the sticker, plus account messages like confirmation codes.
  • Who gets texts: only the person who enters their own mobile number when turning on a sticker, confirms it with a code we text them, and checks the box agreeing to receive texts. We never text the person who scanned the sticker, and agreeing to texts is not required to buy a sticker.
  • How often: message frequency varies with how often people scan your sticker. Each person who scans can send at most 2 messages a day, and you will receive no more than 10 feedback texts a day per sticker.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop: reply STOP to any message to cancel. You'll get one text confirming you're unsubscribed, and then no more messages. Reply START to turn texts back on.
  • For help: reply HELP to any message, or email hello@motherbumpersticker.com.
  • Other replies: reply PAUSE to pause texts for 24 hours, RESUME to restart them, or BLOCK to block the person who sent your most recent message.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Your information is handled as described in our Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How you sign up for texts

Each order comes with an activation card. The parent scans it (or visits motherbumper.co and types the sticker code), enters their mobile number, and checks a box that is not checked in advance. We then text a 6-digit code to confirm the number belongs to them. Texts start only after they confirm.

The agreement reads: "I agree to get texts from Mother Bumper with feedback about this car. Up to a few texts a day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help. See our texting terms and privacy policy."

Sample messages

  • Confirmation code: "Mother Bumper: your code is 482915. It expires in 10 minutes. If you didn't ask for this, ignore this text."
  • Welcome: "Mother Bumper: your sticker for the blue Civic is on! You'll get a text here when someone shares feedback. Reply BLOCK to block a sender, PAUSE to pause 24 hrs, STOP to stop, HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply."
  • Praise: "Mother Bumper: someone gave the blue Civic a thumbs up for good driving at 7:51 AM: "Waited patiently for the kids crossing at the school." Nice work. Reports are anonymous. Reply BLOCK to block this sender."
  • Concern: "Mother Bumper: a driver shared a concern about the blue Civic at 4:09 PM near Main St & 5th: - Following too close. Reports are anonymous and not verified. Reply BLOCK to block this sender, PAUSE to pause texts for 24 hrs."

Feedback is anonymous and not checked

Mother Bumper passes along messages from members of the public. We don't verify whether reports are accurate. Messages are not for emergencies. In an emergency, call 911.

Using a sticker safely

Only scan a sticker when parked or riding as a passenger. Don't use Mother Bumper to harass or threaten anyone. We may block senders, hold messages for review and remove content that breaks these terms. Every message is checked automatically before it's sent: notes with links, phone numbers, emails or rude language are turned back to the sender, and anything that looks threatening is held for a person at Mother Bumper to review.
