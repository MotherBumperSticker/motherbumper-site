Questions

What happens when someone scans the sticker?

They can send feedback about the driver. The message comes through Mother Bumper to the phone number you confirmed when you activated the sticker.

Who receives the messages?

Only the parent who activates the sticker and agrees to receive texts.

What information does the sender see?

None. The person who sends feedback doesn’t see your name, phone number or any information about your child.

How will I know if the reports are accurate?

You won’t. Use them as a conversation starter with your kid.

Where does the location in a message come from?

Location is optional. A sender can add a rough cross street, such as “Main Street and Fifth.” Mother Bumper doesn’t use GPS, track the car or ask for the sender’s location.

Can I reply to the sender?

No. Mother Bumper is designed to pass along a quick message, not start a conversation. You can reply BLOCK to stop messages from that sender or PAUSE to take a 24-hour break.

How do I stop the texts?

Reply STOP at any time. You’ll receive one confirmation message, and then the texts will end.

Are the stickers removable?

Yes. The sticker is made of weatherproof vinyl and can be removed when you’re ready. Removal may vary depending on how long it has been on the vehicle and the condition of the surface.

Which sticker will earn the most groans and eye rolls?

Caution Yellow — Maximum Mortification. It’s bright, bold and very hard to miss. Choose Yellow on Black if your new driver prefers a little plausible deniability.
